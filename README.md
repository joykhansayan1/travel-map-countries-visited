## About the app

An offline world map you fill in by tapping the countries and regions you have been to. Mark a country Visited, Lived or Wishlist; drill into states, provinces and regions inside 50+ countries; see your stats on the Passport screen; spin a 3D globe; share a map card. Trips with dates and notes. 100% offline — no account, no server, nothing leaves the device.

Map boundary data is from the public-domain [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) dataset. Natural Earth depicts borders on a neutral, de-facto basis; the app takes no position on any territorial dispute, and a setting hides disputed-territory shading.
